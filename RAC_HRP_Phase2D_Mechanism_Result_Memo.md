# Phase 2A Post-Mortem — Pipeline-Level Structureless Null: Result

*Executed once against the countersigned pre-specification
`RAC_HRP_Phase2D_MechanismDiagnostic_PreSpec.md` rev.4. Decision rule applied

**Phase 2A remains CLOSED. No admissible gamma.** `cluster_informativeness`

## 1. Result under the frozen rule

**OUTCOME 2 — beyond regime-free mechanics.** The pre-registered condition
(real B_gamma > q97.5 of BOTH A and S, for ALL FOUR gammas) is satisfied:

`Pr(n_gamma < 2) = 0.000` for every environment and gamma; no replication had
undefined timing. Full quantile table in
`outputs/phase2_mechanism/mechanism_null.json`.

The conclusion the rule licenses, and nothing further:

> The observed burstiness exceeds that generated by the frozen pipeline under
> both uncorrelated (A) and static-covariance (S) regime-free inputs.

**The architectural-artefact hypothesis is not supported.** Regime-free pipeline
mechanics — including persistent covariance structure passed through ~96%-
overlapping 504-day estimation windows — do not reproduce the real temporal
clustering at any gamma.

## 2. Three observations that qualify the result

**(a) D does not overlap, so the D-consistency clause does NOT apply.** Real
B_gamma exceeds D's q97.5 at all four gammas, so the pre-spec's optional
"consistent with designed nonstationarity" statement is not available. The
pre-spec anticipated this: D's Markov states last ~100 and ~33 daily
observations, so a 504-day covariance window spans ~3.8 complete cycles and
materially averages the designed regimes away. D is a **high-frequency** positive
control. Its non-overlap therefore does not argue against slower, more persistent
real regimes — those remain **untested**, not excluded.

**(b) The D margins are thin.** +0.006 at gamma = 1.5 and +0.016 at gamma = 0.5,
against quantiles estimated from 500 replications. The A and S margins
(+0.026 to +0.231) are more comfortable and are consistently ordered across all
four gammas. Weight should rest on the A/S comparison, which is what the frozen
rule adjudicates; the D non-overlap should not carry independent weight.

**(c) S produced LESS excess burstiness than A, at every gamma.** S's q97.5 is
below A's at all four thresholds (e.g. 0.2953 vs 0.3921 at gamma = 2.0). This is
contrary to the mechanism hypothesis that motivated S as the adjudicating null:
persistent covariance structure combined with overlapping estimation windows was
the suspected burstiness generator, and it generated less than uncorrelated noise.
Reported as an observation. No explanation is offered; establishing one would
require further work outside this diagnostic.

## 3. What this does and does not establish

Established: comparable burstiness is **not** produced by the frozen trigger
architecture operating on regime-free returns, under either tested regime-free
covariance specification.

Not established: that the real burstiness is regime-driven. Ruling out two
regime-free mechanisms is not the same as demonstrating regimes. The tested
positive control operates at a timescale the estimation window averages over, so
the diagnostic is silent on persistent regimes.

Also unchanged: even if the real burstiness were regime-driven, Phase 2A's
`cluster_informativeness` failure stands independently — triggered rebalances
were not shown to correspond to statistically distinguishable clustering changes.
Bursty timing and informative timing are separate properties, and only the first
now has support.

## 5. Implementation deviations from the signed pre-spec (all output-neutral)

Each alters no statistic, seed, or environment definition. Recorded in the run
manifest; listed here for explicit review.

1. **Environment S is NOT registered in `ENVIRONMENTS`.** The pre-spec said it
   would be. Registration would have (i) silently enrolled S in the frozen
   Phase 0.5 gate via `gate.py:220` (`envs = environments or
   list(ENVIRONMENTS.keys())`), and (ii) perturbed the `.index()`-based seed
   derivations at `condition2_static_vs_erc.py:102` and
   `diagnostic_static_vs_erc.py:69`. S is dispatched directly by the runner;
   nothing in `rac_hrp/nulls/` was edited.
2. **Sigma_0 factorisation is cached.** The naive implementation recomputed a
   pairwise-complete covariance over ~3,000 columns on every replication (~26 s
   each), which dominated runtime. The RNG is consumed only after the
   factorisation, so caching cannot perturb the draws; verified bit-identical by
   `verify_sigma0_cache()`.
3. **A parallel runner was used.** The countersigned serial script is unmodified;
   `run_mechanism_null_parallel.py` is a separate entry point. Identical output is
   guaranteed by construction, not assumed: seeding is per-replication, m(n) is
   computed in the parent from one shared cache, and results are reordered by
   (env, rep) before any statistic is formed. Confirmed by identical real-data
   output across serial and parallel runs on two machines.

An initial serial run was abandoned after ~13 hours (~55% complete) once the
Sigma_0 inefficiency was identified. No partial results from it were used.
