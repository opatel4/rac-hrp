2.  Separation statistic — phase-adjusted (condition 1)
rev.2 maximised the Jaccard overlap over schedule period only, implicitly anchoring every schedule at offset 0. That is a material flaw: a trigger anchored on the opposite phase of a periodic grid scores as well separated while being exactly calendar-driven. The statistic is replaced by a maximum over period and phase:
P(q,r) = { e_j ∈ E : j mod q = r },   r = 0,…,q−1
J*_γ = max over q ∈ {2,3,4,6}, r ∈ {0,…,q−1}  of  |T_γ ∩ P(q,r)| / |T_γ ∪ P(q,r)|

4.  Attributable turnover — defined, and demoted (condition 3)
Operational definition. At each triggered rebalance t, hold everything else fixed and recompute weights under the previous cluster tree:
turnover_attributable(t) = ½ · Σ_i | w_i(new tree, t) − w_i(old tree, t) |
Both weight vectors use the same date, same covariance, same universe, same allocator; they differ only in the tree. The difference therefore isolates turnover caused by re-clustering rather than by drift or universe change. Aggregation: sum over triggered rebalances, annualised by the evaluation span.
Threshold. The 1.5× HRP_periodic_3 multiplier in rev.2 was asserted, not derived, and no simulation or prior justifies it. Rather than defend an arbitrary cutoff or calibrate one after the fact, turnover is demoted from a hard gate to a reported diagnostic. It is reported for every candidate γ and for the comparators, and it informs the interpretation of any result, but it does not pass or fail a candidate. This removes an unjustified decision boundary rather than dressing one up.
5.  R4 ruling — k is an inception-calibrated structural parameter
Ruling adopted: retain the as-built per-run freeze as primary; rename the option accurately; pre-register per-fold re-estimation as a robustness specification.
Primary: mp_k_mode = &quot;fixed_per_run&quot; (renamed from the inaccurate fixed_per_fold). k is taken from the Marchenko-Pastur count of the trailing 504-day covariance ending at the first eligible rebalance and held for the whole run. No look-ahead: no observation after that date enters it.
Rationale: a constant k gives AR a constant mathematical definition, so ΔAR reflects movement in the eigenvalue spectrum and never a discrete change in the component count. Re-estimating k at fold boundaries would itself inject mechanical level shifts into AR.
Description: k is reported as an inception-calibrated structural parameter (k = 15, calibrated at the 2003 inception window), not as a quantity recalibrated each fold. The known weakness — a twenty-year extrapolation from one estimate — is stated rather than obscured.
Robustness (pre-registered): fixed_per_fold re-estimation reported as a separate specification.
Diagnostic only: the time-varying median spec.k of 16 against the frozen 15 is reassuring but is not offered as a justification, since it is an ex-post observation.
6.  Procedure (unchanged in substance, resequenced for clarity)
Step 1. Structural diagnostics only for γ ∈ {0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0}. No returns, Sharpe, or drawdown computed or displayed.
Step 2. Apply the deterministic selection rule automatically in code: among the candidates, select the value closest to the inherited γ = 1.0 passing every hard structural criterion; ties broken toward the larger γ. If none passes, Phase 2 stops.
Step 3. Write the selected γ and the full diagnostic set to a hashed frozen record.
Step 4. Run Null Gate v2 at the selected γ. The existing verdict applies only to γ = 1.0 and is not inherited. On failure, Phase 2 stops (§3).
